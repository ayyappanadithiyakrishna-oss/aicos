# Product

## Register
product

## Users
Active equity and options researchers scanning US large-cap news on desktop and phone.

## Product Purpose
Turn sourced coverage into explainable exposures, measured outcomes and accountable estimates.
The first screen should make it easy to spot a catalyst, see the source, and open a ticker.

## Brand Personality
Precise, calm, candid. Apple system typography and restrained blue interactions, as requested.

## Anti-references
Repeated template prose, long ornamental cards, inflated confidence labels, unqualified live badges.

## Design Principles
- Let real headlines and original publishers lead.
- Give catalysts more prominence than general commentary, while keeping all coverage accessible.
- Preserve visible uncertainty and offer details on demand.
- Keep the interface compact and readable with keyboard and touch support.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA text contrast, explicit labels beyond color, keyboard navigation, reduced motion and transparency.
